Anatomy

Horizontal right-pointing arrowhead with a concave back. Tip apex at x≈77 y≈30.5. The two rear tails carry a soft round to prevent it reading as a generic chevron — it's a mark, not a symbol. Pulled directly from the apostrophe in the Bish's logo. Reads as "go" — the brand's motion-and-direction glyph.

Where it replaces generic UI

Swap in for ›, >, or chevron-right wherever directional affordance is needed: section eyebrows, list bullets, breadcrumb separators, empty-state anchors, pagination chevrons.

Token

SVG path exposed as --wf-path (CSS clip-path) and --wf-viewbox. Use inline SVG in most cases; clip-path on shaped divs only.
