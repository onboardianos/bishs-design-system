Architecture · Three-Tier Drill-Down
Every screen maps to one of three tiers. Users always move summary → operational → detail — never sideways into ambiguity.
Tier 1 · Summary
Orientation
"What needs attention?"
KPI cards · dominant metric · delta · exception alerts. Answers in 2 seconds. Job: signal, not resolve.
Tier 2 · Operational
Context
"Which ones, in what order?"
Queues · comparison · trend modules. Filter pre-applied from the click that arrived here. Job: scope the work.
Tier 3 · Detail
Resolution
"Act on this one."
Drawer (inspect-and-return) or full page (task-switch). Rule: drawer first. Page only when the user is committing to the work itself.
