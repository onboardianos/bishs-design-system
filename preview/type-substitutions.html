Type · Font Substitutions & Rationale
The 2022 brand guide specifies paid, commercially-licensed faces. Nexus substitutes open-licensed, web-ready alternatives that preserve the visual personality — not perfect matches, but compositionally equivalent at UI sizes.
Display Face
Sucrose→Bebas Neue
DEALER OS
Brand-guide Sucrose is an Adobe Fonts stencil-unicase face used in the Bish's logo itself. Bebas preserves the condensed all-caps geometry, tall x-height, and utilitarian-stencil character. Not a perfect match — Sucrose has broken stencil strokes; Bebas is clean — but it holds the same compositional weight at UI sizes.

UI Face
Geogrotesque→Barlow Condensed
WAYFINDER · OPERATIONS QUEUE
Geogrotesque is a premium geometric-condensed sans for subheads and chrome. Barlow Condensed is the closest open-source face — clean letterforms at small sizes, geometric-condensed personality, and the full 300–700 weight range Nexus needs.

Not really a substitution. Source Sans 3 is Adobe's updated variable-font version of Source Sans Pro — same family, same designer, modern format. This one is aligned with the brand guide, not replaced.

Why substitute rather than license the real faces?
Cost at scaleSucrose and Geogrotesque are paid per-site or per-pageview. Across Dealer OS, the portal, and admin tools — thousands of employees and tens of thousands of customer-facing pageviews — license cost compounds. Bebas and Barlow are free, forever.
PerformanceGoogle Fonts' Bebas and Barlow are CDN-cached and compressed; they load faster than foundry-delivered fonts, and the browser cache is reused across any other site the user visits that also serves them.
Fallback safetyFree Google fonts have guaranteed long-term availability. Paid foundry fonts can hit license disputes, acquisition drama, or CDN changes that break production overnight.
